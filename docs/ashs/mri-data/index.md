# MRI Data

ASHS atlases are trained on MRI scans acquired with a particular protocol, and they work best on scans that look similar. Before running ASHS, check that your data meet the requirements of the atlas you plan to use:

- [ASHS-PMC Atlas Requirements](pmc-atlas.md): T2-weighted MRI (about 0.4 × 0.4 × 2.0 mm³) plus whole-brain T1-weighted MRI, for hippocampal subfields and MTL cortical subregions.
- [ASHS-PMC-T1 Atlas Requirements](pmc-t1-atlas.md): whole-brain T1-weighted MRI only, for anterior/posterior hippocampus and MTL cortical subregions.

The [ADNI3 Data](adni3.md) page describes how to obtain high-resolution T2-weighted MTL scans and ASHS-derived measures from the Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative.
