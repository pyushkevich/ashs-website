## ASHS Classic

Please cite this paper for the ASHS software and the **UPenn PMC atlas**:

- Yushkevich PA, Pluta J, Wang H, Ding SL, Xie L, Gertje E, Mancuso L, Kliot D, Das SR and Wolk DA. Automated Volumetry and Regional Thickness Analysis of Hippocampal Subfields and Medial Temporal Cortical Structures in Mild Cognitive Impairment. *Human Brain Mapping* 36(1):258–287, 2015. [PubMed 25181316](http://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed/25181316)

Please cite these papers when using the **ASHS-PMC-T1 atlas**:

- Xie L, Wisse LEM, Manjón JV, Wang H, Das SR, Wolk DA, Yushkevich PA. [Accounting for the Confound of Meninges in Segmenting Entorhinal and Perirhinal Cortices in T1-weighted MRI.](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-319-46723-8_65) *Medical Image Computing and Computer-Assisted Intervention – MICCAI 2016*, pp. 564–571. Springer International Publishing.

- Wisse LE, Kuijf HJ, Honingh AM, Wang H, Pluta JB, Das SR, Wolk DA, Zwanenburg JJ, Yushkevich PA and Geerlings MI. Automated hippocampal subfield segmentation at 7T MRI. *American Journal of Neuroradiology* 37(6):1050–1057, 2016. [PubMed 26846925](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed/26846925)
