# Related Projects

## Related software

- [CRASHS](https://github.com/pyushkevich/crashs): surface-based modeling and cortical thickness mapping of the MTL from ASHS and HyperASHS segmentations
- [ITK-SNAP](http://itksnap.org/): useful for QC, viewing and editing ASHS and HyperASHS segmentations (version 4.6 or later for HyperASHS results)
- [Greedy](https://sites.google.com/view/greedyreg/home): image registration used by fast-ASHS and HyperASHS
- [Convert3D](http://itksnap.org/c3d): a "Swiss Army knife" command-line image processing tool
- [nnU-Net](https://github.com/MIC-DKFZ/nnUNet): the deep learning segmentation framework underlying HyperASHS
- [Joint Label Fusion](http://www.nitrc.org/projects/picsl_malf/): the multi-atlas segmentation technology underlying ASHS Classic

## Related projects

- [Hippocampal Subfields Group (HSG)](https://hippocampalsubfields.com/): a community effort to develop a harmonized hippocampal subfield segmentation protocol
- [Penn Hippocampus Atlas](http://picsl.upenn.edu/pm-hf-atlas/): postmortem MRI and histology of the hippocampus
