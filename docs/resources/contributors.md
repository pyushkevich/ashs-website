# Contributors

ASHS and HyperASHS are the work of many people. Key contributors include:

## Software

- **Paul Yushkevich**: creator and lead developer of ASHS, HyperASHS and CRASHS
- **Yue Li**: lead developer of HyperASHS
- **Long Xie**: ASHS development and the ASHS-PMC-T1 atlas
- **Hongzhi Wang**: joint label fusion and corrective learning, the core of ASHS

- **Pulkit Khandelwal** and **Rohit Jena**: HyperASHS

- **Song-Lin Ding**: neuroanatomy and the PMC segmentation protocol
- **John Pluta**: early ASHS development and the PMC atlas
- **Laura Wisse**: 7T Utrecht atlas, ASHS-PMC-T1 atlas and HyperASHS
- **Niyousha Sadeghpour**: anterior MTL segmentation protocol used by the HyperASHS PMC models
- **Amanda Denning**: HyperASHS atlases
- **Robin de Flores**: ASHS-PMC-T1 atlas
- **David Berron**: ABC 3T and 7T and Magdeburg 7T atlases
- **Ana Daugherty**, **Valerie Carr** and the [Hippocampal Subfields Group](https://hippocampalsubfields.com/): harmonized hippocampal body protocol used by the HSG_Body_Atlas model
